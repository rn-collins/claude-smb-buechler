What it actually is

Claude for Small Business is a package of connectors and ready-to-run workflows that put Claude inside the tools you already use every day.1 Anthropic launched it on May 13, 2026.1

Three starter workflows

Tap to copy, paste into Claude, and adjust in plain language.1

1 · Cash + invoices — Monday reset

Pull my cash position from QuickBooks and reconcile it against my PayPal settlements. Rank any overdue invoices oldest to newest, and draft a friendly reminder email for each, but don't send anything until I approve it.

2 · Close the month

Reconcile QuickBooks against PayPal for last month, flag any mismatches, and write a plain-English profit-and-loss summary I can forward to my accountant.

3 · Morning brief

Each morning, pull my cash from QuickBooks, settlements from PayPal, and pipeline from HubSpot, and post the three things that need my attention today to Slack.

What to connect first

Tap each step as you go.

  • ✓Install the Claude desktop app and turn on Claude for Small Business in Cowork2
  • ✓Connect the one tool where your work piles up, often QuickBooks or PayPal
  • ✓Run one small workflow end-to-end, approving each step the first time
  • ✓Only then connect a second tool and chain them together
  • ✓Keep “approve before it sends” on until you trust a workflow1
